Permanent DNS Auto-Fix — macOS LaunchDaemon

2026-09-18 LaunchDaemon Resolved

The recurring "DNS broke again" failure was a stale mDNSResponder cache after boot / Wi-Fi reconnect — not reverted settings. Delivered a root LaunchDaemon that re-asserts public DNS and flushes the cache at boot and every 30 minutes, removing all manual intervention.
